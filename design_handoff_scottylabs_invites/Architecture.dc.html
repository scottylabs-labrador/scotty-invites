Committee scoping, everywhere

Every admin belongs to a committee — Foundry, Tech, Design, and the rest — or posts all-club as ScottyLabs; creating an event stamps events.committee_id. Dashboards, CSV exports, and pass stamps all filter on it — super admins see every committee, admins see their own.

Answers stay queryable

Standard fields land in typed columns; per-event custom answers land in answers.value jsonb keyed by question. One COPY gives a committee its whole event as CSV — no spreadsheet chasing.

Status digests, on your schedule

Each event lists an updates_email; Mailgun sends it an hourly, daily, or weekly digest of new signups and reviews waiting — anything pending over 24 hours escalates immediately. The pass's contact_email is separate, so guests always reach a human.
